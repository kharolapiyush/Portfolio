Case Study · 03

NFC Attendance Pro

Records attendance from NFC scans with webcam verification, live updates, and PDF reports.

FocusAttendance · NFC & Computer Vision

The Problem

Classroom attendance needs both a quick check-in flow and records that are easy to review later.

The Approach

  • Record student attendance using NFC.
  • Use OpenCV for webcam verification.
  • Expose Flask REST APIs and send live updates through Flask-SocketIO.
  • Process records with Pandas and generate PDF reports with ReportLab.

Architecture

The attendance flow combines NFC scans and webcam verification. Flask exposes the records through REST APIs. Flask-SocketIO sends updates, while Pandas and ReportLab support reporting.
